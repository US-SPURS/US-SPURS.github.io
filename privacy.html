Privacy Policy

The US Department of SPURS is committed to protecting your privacy and securing your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website.

Last Updated: December 2025

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of visit
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website

This information is used solely for website analytics, security monitoring, and improving user experience.

Information You Provide

If you choose to contact us through our website, you may provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message content

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Monitor website security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Generate aggregate statistics about website usage

Information Sharing and Disclosure

The US Department of SPURS does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information:

  • With other federal agencies as required for official government business
  • When required by law, court order, or legal process
  • To protect national security or public safety
  • With contractors or service providers working on behalf of the government (under strict confidentiality agreements)

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics services that compromise your privacy.

Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality
  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

Security Measures

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Restricted access to personal information
  • Secure data storage and transmission protocols

Despite these measures, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to our website.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any website you visit.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access information we have collected about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out of certain data collection practices

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post any changes on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Legal Authority

Collection and use of personal information is authorized under relevant federal statutes and regulations governing federal agency operations and privacy protection, including:

  • Privacy Act of 1974
  • E-Government Act of 2002
  • Federal Information Security Management Act (FISMA)
  • Other applicable federal laws and regulations
